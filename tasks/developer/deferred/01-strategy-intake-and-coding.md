# Deferred — strategy intake and coding

## Depends on

The trading partner supplying a strategy PDF and clarifying its instrument, entry, exit, stop-loss, take-profit, and risk rules.

## Developer work after receipt

- Convert the human strategy into a versioned coded strategy.
- Produce a human-readable rule checklist for both-owner approval.
- Add deterministic fixtures, backtests, and paper-execution tests.
- Do not activate the strategy until both owners approve it.
